## Ingredients

- 1 (14 or 16 oz) block **extra firm tofu**
- 1 tablespoon **avocado oil**
- 1/2 teaspoon **onion powder**
- 1/2 teaspoon **garlic powder**
- 1/2 teaspoon **salt**
- **black pepper** _to preference_
- 2 tablespoons **corn starch**

----

## Instructions

### Press the Tofu

1. Ahead of time, use your favorite method to press your block of tofu for at least 10 minutes.

### Make the Tofu

1. Preheat air fryer to **400°F**.
2. Cut the pressed tofu into roughly 1" cubes, or size of choice.
3. Place the **tofu pieces** in a mixing bowl. Drizzle on the **avocado oil**, then sprinkle over the **onion powder**, **garlic powder**, **salt**, and **black pepper**. Give the pieces a toss to coat — try to be gentle so the tofu pieces don't crumble. A flexible rubber spatula can be really helpful here. (Or, if you have a container with a lid, you can simply give everything a gentle shake to coat.)
4. With the tofu pieces coated in oil and seasonings, now add the **cornstarch** and give the tofu another gentle toss to coat.
5. Transfer the tofu pieces into the basket of your air fryer. It's best if you can keep the pieces in one layer with just a little bit of space in between them (so they don't stick together), but it's okay if they overlap a little.
6. Air fry for **18-20 minutes at 400°F**, or until golden brown and crispy. Give the air fryer basket a shake at around the 8 minute mark. Then give it another shake after 5 more minutes. This will help the tofu to brown and crisp evenly.
