---
title: 'Bean Burritos'
---

## Ingredients

- 5-6 large **flour tortillas**
- **Ortega taco sauce** _dizzled on to of the buritos to preference_

### Burrito Filling

- 1 can **vegetarian refried beans**
- 1 can **reduced sodium black beans**
- 1 cup **shredded Mexican cheese**
- 1/2 **onion** _finely diced_
- 1 **bell pepper** _finely diced_
- 1 cup **salsa** _mild, or heat to preference_
- 1 teaspoon **garlic powder**
- 1 teaspoon **onion powder**
- 1 teaspoon **cumin**
- 1 teaspoon **chili powder**
- 1 teaspoon **salt**
- a shake of **cayenne**
- a shake of **paprika**

----

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375. And spray or lighly coat an oven safe dish in **oil**.
2. Add the **filling ingredients** to a large mixing bowl, stir together.
3. Fill each **tortilla** with the filling, roll the tortilla into a burrito, and add it to the dish.
4. Drizzle the **taco sauce** on top of the burritos, and sprinkle with some extra **shredded cheese**.
5. Bake for 22-25 minutes.
