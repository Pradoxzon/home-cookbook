## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons **olive oil**
- 1 medium **yellow onion** _diced_
- 2 medium **carrots** _julienned or shredded, or handful baby carrots chopped_
- 2 **celery ribs** _thinly sliced_
- 1 small to medium **zucchini** _sliced and quartered_
- 6 cloves **garlic** _minced_
- 2 **bay leaves**
- 2 tablespoons **nutritional yeast** _optional, to taste_
- 1 tablespoon **Italian seasoning**
- 1/2 tablespoon **dried oregano**
- 1/4 teaspoon **salt** _adjust to taste_
- 1/4 teaspoon **black pepper** _adjust to taste_
- 1/8 teaspoon **read pepper flakes** _optional_
- 4 cups **vegetable broth**
- 2 cups **water**
- 28 oz canned **diced tomatoes**
- 2 tablespoons **tomato paste**
- 15 oz canned **great northern beans** _drained and rinsed_
- 15 oz canned **kidney beans** _drained and rinsed_
- 1 1/2 cups **small shell pasta**
- 2 cups **spinach** _chopped_
- 2 tablespoons **fresh lemon juice** _optional_
- **fresh parsley** _topped when serving, optional_
- **grated parmesan cheese** _topped when serving, to preference_

----

## Instructions

1. In a large heavy bottomed pot, over medium heat, add the **oil**. Once heated, add in **onions**, **celery**, **carrots**, and **zucchini**. Saute for 3-5 minutes or until the vegetables are softened.
2. Add **garlic**, stir, and cook for 30 more seconds.
3. Add **bay leaves**, **nutritional yeast**, **Italian seasoning**, **oregano**, **salt**, **pepper**, and **red pepper flakes**. Stir together and let cook for 30 seconds.
4. Add **vegetable broth**, **water**, **tomatoes**, and **tomato paste** to the pot. Bring to a simmer.
5. Add the **white beans**, **kidney beans**, and **pasta**. Allow to simmer for 12-15 minutes or until pasta is cooked.
6. Add **greens** and **lemon juice** and allow to cook until softened and wilted (about 4-5 minutes).
7. Taste and add more seasoning as desired. Remove from heat and serve topped with **fresh parsley** and **parmesan** on top.
