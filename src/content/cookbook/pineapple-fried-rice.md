## Ingredients

- 1 batch **crispy air fryer tofu**
- 3 tablespoons **vegetable oil** _or butter_
- 2 teaspoons **sambal chili paste** _more or less to taste_
- **black pepper** _to taste_
- 4 **green onions** _thinly sliced, green and white parts separated_
- 4-6 cloves **garlic** _minced_
- 3 cups **cold leftover rice**
- 1 cup **frozen mixed peas and carrots**
- 2 tablespoons **soy sauce**
- 1 small can **pineapple tidbits** _drained_

----

## Instructions

### Prep

1. This recipe moves fast once you start, so make sure all your ingredients are prepped and easy to access.

### Fried Rice

1. Heat **vegetable oil** over medium-high heat. Add in the sliced **white parts of the green onions**, along with **chili paste** (if using), and **black pepper**. Stir-fry for 2-3 minutes, or until the scallions start to look translucent.
2. Add in the **minced garlic** and stir fry for another minute. Reduce heat slightly if the garlic starts to brown.
3. Add in the **frozen peas and carrots** and stir-fry for another 1-2 minutes to warm through.
4. Add in the **cold leftover rice**. Stir-fry the rice for 4-6 minutes. It should start to take on some golden-brown color.
5. Add in the **soy sauce** and stir to distribute.
6. Fold in the **tofu**, **pineapple**, and the **green parts of the scallions** and stir-fry for an additional 2-3 minutes to heat everything through.
