## Ingredients

- 1/2 pound **wide noodles** _rice or wheat_

### Maple Toasted Peanuts

- 2 tablespoons **toasted sesame oil**
- 1-2 tablespoons **maple syrup**
- 1/2 cup **roasted unsalted peanuts**
- 2 tablespoons **sesame seeds**
- 1/2 teaspoon **red pepper flakes** _adjust to taste_

### Tofu and Vegetables

- 3/4 cup **low sodium soy sauce**
- 1/3 cup **maple syrup**
- 2 tablespoons **ketchup** _or 2 tablespoons tomato paste_
- 2 tablespoons **fish sauce**
- 3-4 tablespoons **Thai red curry paste**
- 1 block **super firm tofu** _cubed_
- 6-8 cloves **garlic** _minced_
- 1 inch **fresh ginger** _grated_
- 1 **yellow squash** _sliced_
- 1 **red bell pepper** _sliced_
- 1 cup **fresh basil**
- 1 **lime** _zested and juiced_

----

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add **noodles** and cook according to package directions. Drain.

### Maple Toasted Peanuts

1. In a large skillet over medium heat, add **sesame oil** and **maple syrup**, stir together. Add the **peanuts** and stir. Cooked for 2 minutes.
2. Add the **sesame seeds** and **red pepper flakes**. Cook another 1-2, stirring often. Careful not to burn.
3. Put just the peanuts on a parchment lined baking pan and set aside.

### Tofu and Vegetables

1. In a small bowl, combine **soy sauce**, **maple syrup**, **Thai red curry paste**, **ketchup**, and **fish sauce**.
2. In the same skillet from the peanuts: add the **tofu cubes**. Stir together and let cook for 3-4 minutes flipping to get each side golden brown.
3. Add in **ginger** and **garlic** and stir together. Cook 1 more minutes.
4. Add in half the sauce. Stir together well. Let cook for 2-3 minutes.
5. Add in **peppers** and **squash** and stir. Cook for 3-4 minutes.
6. Add in **cooked noodles**, remaining sauce, **basil**, **lime juice** and **zest**. Serve with the **toasted peanuts**, additional **basil**, and **lime juice** if needed.
